# KAbot

AI-assisted planning and content creation for K-12 educators in Canada.

KAbot helps teachers generate lesson plans, quizzes, and worksheets grounded in
provincial curricula, and manage their classes, gradebook, attendance, and calendar.
School administrators onboard teachers and oversee accounts from a dedicated portal.

## What teachers can do

- Generate lesson plans, quizzes, and worksheets aligned to Canadian provincial curricula
- Manage class rosters, track attendance, and maintain a gradebook
- Schedule with a shared class calendar
- Submit and track support requests from within the platform
